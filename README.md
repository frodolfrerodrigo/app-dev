# 🎬 My Favorite Movies and Series

## 🍔 **Sausage Party**
![Sausage Party Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/5/5e/Sausage_Party_%28film%29.png)
**Genre:** Animation, Comedy, Adventure  
**Director:** Greg Tiernan & Conrad Vernon  

> “A sausage strives to discover the truth about his existence.”

**Why I like it:**  
- Funny and creative take on life in a supermarket.  
- Has hidden adult humor and philosophical themes.  
- Voice actors like Seth Rogen and Jonah Hill make it hilarious.  

---

## 🦸‍♂️ **Lastikman**
![Lastikman Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/07/Lastikman2003poster.jpg)
**Genre:** Action, Sci-Fi, Superhero  
**Origin:** Philippine Comic Character by Mars Ravelo  

> “Ang lalaking kayang humaba at bumalik sa dati — Lastikman!”

**Cool Facts:**  
- Can stretch his body like rubber.  
- Symbol of Filipino heroism.  
- Adapted into movies and TV series.  

**Favorite Version:**  
- *Vhong Navarro’s* portrayal in the 2004 film — full of humor and heart.  

---

## 💪 **Captain Barbell**
![Captain Barbell Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/5/5d/Captain_Barbell_TV_Series.jpg)
**Genre:** Action, Fantasy, Superhero  
**Created by:** Mars Ravelo  

> “Taas kamao, ibaba ang kasamaan!”

**About the Story:**  
- An ordinary man gains super strength through a magical barbell.  
- Similar to *Superman* but with a Filipino twist.  

**I like it because:**  
- Inspiring story about courage and power.  
- Shows that heroes can come from humble beginnings.
